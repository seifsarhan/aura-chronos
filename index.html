Our Philosophy

Where Mechanical Precision Meets Living Art

Founded in 1924, Aura Chronos stands at the intersection of Swiss chronometric engineering and modern architectural luxury. Every bridge is hand-beveled, every escapement regulated across five positions, and every vault acquisition certified by master horologists.

  • COSC Chronometer Precision: Tested for 15 consecutive days across temperature extremes.
  • Authenticated Provenance: Digital & archival certificate included with every serial number.
  • Armored White-Glove Delivery: Complimentary insured courier across Cairo, Geneva & London.
